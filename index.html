Withdrawal Sequence

WealthMap uses a simplified withdrawal sequence to fund retirement spending:

  • Cash reserves
  • Taxable brokerage accounts
  • Traditional IRA and 401(k) accounts
  • Roth IRA accounts

Required Minimum Distributions (RMDs) are taken when applicable and are shown separately.

During positive market years, the model may replenish cash reserves from brokerage assets based on your Cash Reserve target setting.

This withdrawal sequence is a planning assumption and may not be optimal for every tax situation.
